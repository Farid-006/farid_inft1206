This is a fact file about bears and their mating rituals.

Bears are known for being romantic creatures. During mating season, male bears will go out of their way to attract a female. Wild bears have been observed decorating their caves with paintings and arranging beds of reeds to impress potential mates.

Urban bears take a more modern approach. They have been seen setting up mood lighting in their apartments and playing Michael Bublé CDs to create a romantic atmosphere.

Experts say that bears are monogamous by nature and prefer to find a single partner for life. Dr. Jane Wilson, a bear researcher at the University of Wild Animals, states that bears show a level of romantic dedication that is rarely seen in the animal kingdom.

Whether wild or urban, bears put a lot of effort into their relationships, making them one of the most romantic species on the planet.
